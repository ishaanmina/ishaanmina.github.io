A _dhaba_ is the cheap roadside eatery you find on every Indian highway — no frills, open to anyone, and
you leave fed. _Gyan_ is knowledge. **Gyan da Dhaba** is exactly what it sounds like: a roadside stop for
knowledge, where anyone can pull in and take what they need.

Study material has a distribution problem rather than a scarcity problem. Notes and past papers exist, but
they sit in private WhatsApp groups, in one senior's folder, or behind a login — which means access depends
on who you happen to know.

Gyandadhabha was my attempt at fixing that locally: a single free, central place collecting notes and past
papers for students in my city, for friends, and for anyone else who wanted to learn. No account, no fee,
no gatekeeping.

- Built and hosted as a static site, so it stays free to run and cannot quietly disappear behind a paywall.
- Organised around the thing students actually search for — the paper or the topic — rather than around who
  contributed it.
