**IBM Qiskit Global Summer School** · 2021 · with Amira Abbas

Current quantum hardware is noisy enough that even trivial arithmetic comes back wrong most of the time.
Error mitigation is the practical response — you accept the noise and correct for it statistically rather
than waiting for hardware good enough not to need it.

- Completed IBM's officially certified Qiskit Global Summer School programme on Quantum Computing and
  Quantum Machine Learning, in Python and **Qiskit**.
- Built and submitted a quantum error-mitigation solution that reduced quantum computational error from
  roughly **70% down to under 10%** on a basic arithmetic verification task.

Code for this and my subsequent quantum work lives in the
[QIC repository](https://github.com/ishaanmina/QIC).
