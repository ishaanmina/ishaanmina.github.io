**Physics and Astronomy Society, IIT Delhi** · 2023 · Founding member and instructor

The society had run observation sessions and outreach for years but had never built anything that left the
ground. I laid the foundation for the university's first student-led space and rocketry project, which meant
starting with people who had never instrumented a test before and working toward a vehicle that could
reliably reach a target altitude.

### Teaching the fundamentals first

Most of the early project was instruction, because you cannot delegate work on a solid motor to someone who
has not yet measured a strain. I taught juniors:

- **How a strain gauge works** — why a change in resistance maps to mechanical strain, how to bridge it, and
  how to trust the number that comes out.
- **How to build a test bench** — static firing needs a rig that constrains the motor, isolates the load
  path into the sensor, and fails safely. Designing the bench is a genuine engineering problem in its own
  right, and it is where the strain gauge work becomes real.

### Propellant

We worked with **potassium nitrate (KNO₃) and a sugar fuel** — glucose and sucrose — a well-documented
solid propellant chemistry that is accessible to a student team while still being a real energetic material.
The work was in the combinations: the oxidiser-to-fuel ratio sets burn rate and specific impulse, and
characterising that trade-off on the test bench is what turns a chemistry recipe into a predictable motor.

### Airframe and the altitude problem

- **Body design** — building an airframe that survives the thrust load and the aerodynamic loads without
  carrying dead mass it does not need.
- **Alignment** — measuring and correcting for it, since a motor whose thrust vector is not through the
  centre of mass will not fly straight regardless of how good the propellant is.
- **Mass budgeting for altitude** — working backwards from a target to the mass we could afford: first
  100 ft, then 1000 ft, then further. This is the constraint that drives every other decision, and it is
  the cleanest possible demonstration to a junior of why engineering is mostly budgets.

### Skills

Solid propellant chemistry · strain gauge instrumentation · test bench design · structural design ·
