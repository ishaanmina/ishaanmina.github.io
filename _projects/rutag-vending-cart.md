**Rural Technology Action Group, IIT Delhi** · July – August 2021

Street vegetable vendors work from a flat platform on four wheels, and the commercial upgrades that exist —
powered brakes, cold storage, weighing and payment systems — are priced far beyond what the vendors
actually earn. The project started from the vendors rather than from the technology.

### Work

- Conducted **30+ on-ground surveys** of vegetable cart owners and vendors to identify real needs and pain
  points in their daily operations and finances.
- Translated the survey findings into a modular cart-attachment design specification, with each attachment
  addressing a distinct identified need.
- Created precise **SolidWorks** CAD of the standard existing cart, with accurate dimensions and material
  properties, to calculate centre of mass and simulate the behaviour of castor wheels, power brakes and
  other proposed solutions.
- Estimated the upgraded modular system would cost **40–60% less** than comparable existing commercial
  solutions.

### Status

Design and specification phase complete — CAD, material selection and manufacturing method are documented.
Manufacturing and deployment were outside the scope of this phase.

### Skills

User research and requirements gathering · SolidWorks CAD · centre-of-mass and stability analysis · modular design · material selection · design for cost
